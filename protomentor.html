About ProtoMentor

ProtoMentor is a pioneering student innovation platform that transforms raw ideas into tangible solutions. We're more than mentors - we're your collaborators in the innovation journey.

Our unique approach combines technical expertise with entrepreneurial thinking, helping students navigate from concept to prototype to market-ready product. Whether you're preparing for a hackathon, academic project, or startup venture, we provide the tools and knowledge to succeed.

With a network of industry professionals, academic experts, and successful student innovators, we've created an ecosystem where ideas flourish and dreams become reality.
